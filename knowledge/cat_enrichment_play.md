# Cat Play and Enrichment

Play is a daily need for cats, not an optional extra.

## Play
- Cats are sprinters rather than marathon runners: play comes in short bursts, and even one minute of frenzied activity is a good result. Kitten play may last only a few minutes.
- Choose a time to play that suits both of you, and play every day.
- Wand toys (a rod with a string and a small toy at the end) suit social play with a person.
- Let the cat complete the hunting sequence, including the "catch", and finish the game on a positive note, for example by switching from a laser pointer to a wand toy.
- Kittens should avoid toys made of ribbon or string, which can cause serious intestinal problems if swallowed.

## Environment
- Play areas work best when they offer hiding opportunities and access to high places.

## Suggested tasks
- Interactive play session, 10 minutes, daily, medium priority.
- Rotate toys or refresh enrichment, 10 minutes, weekly, low priority.
