# Cat Play and Enrichment

Indoor cats need daily activity to stay at a healthy weight and avoid boredom-related behavior such as scratching furniture, overgrooming or nighttime yowling.

## Play
- Aim for at least two interactive play sessions per day of about 10-15 minutes each. Wand toys that mimic prey work well.
- Let the cat "catch" the toy at the end to complete the hunting sequence, then offer a small meal or treat.
- Rotate toys so they stay interesting. Never let a cat play unsupervised with string or ribbon, which can be swallowed.

## Environment
- Provide scratching posts (tall, sturdy, in a social area), perches or cat trees, and window views.
- Hiding spots help cats feel secure, especially in multi-pet homes.
- Puzzle feeders turn mealtime into enrichment.

## Suggested tasks
- Interactive play session, 15 minutes, daily, medium priority.
- Evening play session, 10 minutes, daily, medium priority.
- Rotate toys or refresh enrichment, 10 minutes, weekly, low priority.
