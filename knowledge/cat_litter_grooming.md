# Cat Litter Box and Grooming

## Litter box
- If you use clumping litter, remove feces and clumps daily and add clean litter as needed.
- Change the litter often enough that it looks and smells dry and clean. The more cats using the box, the more often this is needed.
- Keep as many litter boxes as cats in the house, plus one.
- Cats prefer to eat and eliminate in separate areas, so keep boxes away from food.

## Grooming (International Cat Care)
- Aim to groom your cat at least once a week.
- Longhaired cats need to be groomed at least daily.

## Suggested tasks
- Scoop litter box, 5 minutes, daily, high priority.
- Full litter change and box wash, 20 minutes, weekly, medium priority.
- Brush coat, 10 minutes, weekly (daily for long-haired cats), low priority.
