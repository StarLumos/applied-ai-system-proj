# Cat Litter Box and Grooming

## Litter box
- Scoop at least once a day. Cats may avoid a dirty box.
- Fully empty, wash with mild unscented soap and refill about every 1-2 weeks (clumping litter) or more often for non-clumping litter.
- Provide one box per cat plus one extra, placed in quiet, easy-to-reach spots away from food and water.
- A sudden change in litter habits, straining or blood in urine needs prompt veterinary attention, especially in male cats.

## Brushing
- Short-haired cats benefit from weekly brushing. Long-haired cats need brushing several times a week, ideally daily, to prevent mats.

## Nails and teeth
- Trim nails every 2-3 weeks if the cat does not wear them down.
- Dental disease is common in cats. Brush teeth with cat toothpaste if your cat allows, and ask your vet about dental checks.

## Suggested tasks
- Scoop litter box, 5 minutes, daily, high priority.
- Full litter change and box wash, 20 minutes, weekly, medium priority.
- Brush coat, 10 minutes, weekly (daily for long-haired cats), low priority.
- Nail trim, 10 minutes, every 2-3 weeks, low priority.
