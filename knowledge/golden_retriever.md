# Golden Retriever Breed Care

Golden Retrievers are friendly, energetic, large sporting dogs, typically 55-75 pounds, with a life expectancy of about 10-12 years.

## Exercise
- They need at least about an hour of activity daily, and many do well with more. They love fetch and swimming.
- They are eager to please and respond well to training, which also gives them mental exercise.
- Bored goldens can become mouthy or destructive.

## Grooming
- They have a water-repellent double coat and shed year-round, with heavier shedding in spring and fall.
- Brush several times a week, and daily during heavy shedding.
- Check and clean ears regularly. Their floppy ears and love of water make ear infections common.
- Trim nails every 3-4 weeks.

## Health considerations
- Prone to hip and elbow dysplasia, so avoid excessive high-impact exercise in puppies while joints are developing.
- Prone to obesity. Measure meals and limit treats, since many goldens will eat whatever they are offered.
- Higher than average rates of certain cancers and heart conditions, so regular vet checkups matter.
- Prone to skin allergies and hot spots.

## Suggested tasks
- Morning walk, 30-45 minutes, daily, high priority.
- Fetch or swim session, 30 minutes, daily, medium priority.
- Brush coat, 15 minutes, several times a week, medium priority.
- Ear check, 5 minutes, weekly, medium priority.
