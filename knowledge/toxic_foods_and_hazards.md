# Toxic Foods and Household Hazards

## Foods to avoid
- Chocolate, coffee and caffeine.
- Grapes and raisins, which can lead to kidney damage in dogs.
- Xylitol, a sweetener found in gum, candy, baked goods and toothpaste. It can cause low blood sugar and liver damage.
- Onions, garlic and chives. They can cause anemia, and cats are more susceptible.
- Alcohol. Never give a pet alcohol.
- Raw yeast dough, which can rise in the stomach, causing gas and bloat, and produces alcohol.
- Macadamia nuts (toxic to dogs).
- Raw bones can cause injury or intestinal blockage.

## Lilies and cats
- Lilies are toxic to cats, and the clinical sign listed by the ASPCA is kidney failure.

## If your pet may have eaten something toxic
- Note how much was eaten and contact your veterinarian or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center, a 24-hour emergency hotline, at (888) 426-4435. A consultation fee may apply.

## Suggested tasks
- Check the home and floors for hazards, 10 minutes, weekly, low priority.
