# Toxic Foods and Household Hazards

## Dangerous for dogs and cats
- Chocolate and cocoa (darker chocolate is more dangerous).
- Grapes and raisins (can cause kidney failure in dogs).
- Xylitol, an artificial sweetener found in some sugar-free gum, candy, baked goods and peanut butter. It can cause a dangerous drop in blood sugar and liver damage.
- Onions, garlic, chives and leeks.
- Alcohol, caffeine and raw yeast dough.
- Macadamia nuts (dogs).
- Cooked bones, which can splinter.

## Especially dangerous for cats
- Lilies (including Easter, tiger and day lilies). Even small amounts of pollen or vase water can cause fatal kidney failure.
- Essential oils and some plants. Check any plant against the ASPCA's toxic plant list before bringing it home.

## Other hazards
- Human medications, rodent poison, antifreeze and some cleaning products.

## If your pet may have eaten something toxic
- Call your veterinarian or an animal poison hotline right away, rather than waiting for symptoms. The ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center is available 24/7 (a consultation fee may apply).
- Do not induce vomiting unless a professional tells you to.

## Suggested tasks
- Safety check of the home and floor for hazards, 10 minutes, weekly, low priority.
