# Dog Nail Care

- Most dogs need to have their nails trimmed approximately once a month.
- A second VCA page says most dogs need nail trims every 3-4 weeks, and some need a trim weekly.
- Dogs frequently walked on pavement or concrete may be able to go longer between trims. Inactive dogs may need trims more frequently, such as every three weeks.
- If you can hear your dog's nails clicking on hard floors, they are too long.
- Vet clinics also do nail trims and can offer sedation if your dog is very upset by the procedure.

## Suggested tasks
These are suggested defaults, not statements from the sources.
- Nail trim, 15 minutes, every 3-4 weeks, low priority.
