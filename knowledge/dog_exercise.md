# Dog Exercise Needs

How much exercise a dog needs varies by breed, health and energy level. High-energy breeds need considerably more than low-energy ones, and the right amount for any one dog should be confirmed with a vet.

## Guidelines
- Puppies do better with several short walks or play sessions through the day than one long walk, and should not be over-worked while their bones are still growing.
- Mental stimulation (puzzle feeders, scent games, short training sessions) is an important complement to physical exercise, but not a replacement for it.
- Dogs that do not get enough exercise can become bored and develop unwanted behavior.
- Common rule of thumb (not from the AKC page; treat as a rough starting point): many healthy adult dogs do well with somewhere between 30 minutes and 2 hours of activity a day.

## Suggested tasks
- Morning walk, 30 minutes, daily, high priority.
- Evening walk, 20-30 minutes, daily, medium priority.
- Play or training session, 15 minutes, daily, low priority.
