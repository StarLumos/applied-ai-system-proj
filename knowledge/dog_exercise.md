# Dog Exercise Needs

Most healthy adult dogs need somewhere between 30 minutes and 2 hours of physical activity per day. The right amount depends on breed, age, size and health. Sporting, herding and working breeds usually need the most. Small and toy breeds often need less but still benefit from daily walks.

## Guidelines
- Split exercise into two or more sessions (for example a morning and an evening walk) rather than one long outing.
- Mix activity types: walking, fetch, swimming, sniffing walks and training games.
- Mental exercise counts too. Puzzle feeders, scent games and short training sessions help burn energy.
- Avoid hard exercise right after meals, and take extra care in hot or humid weather. Walk in the cool of the morning or evening and check pavement temperature with your hand.
- Dogs that do not get enough exercise can become bored and develop destructive behavior, barking or weight gain.

## Suggested tasks
- Morning walk, 30 minutes, daily, high priority.
- Evening walk, 20-30 minutes, daily, medium priority.
- Play or training session, 15 minutes, daily, low priority.
