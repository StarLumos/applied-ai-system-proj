# Dog Vaccinations and Preventive Care

## Vaccines (VCA, citing AAHA)
- Core vaccines are canine adenovirus-2 (hepatitis), canine distemper, canine parvovirus, rabies, and leptospirosis. Parainfluenza is core when given in combination with distemper and parvovirus.
- Non-core vaccines listed are Bordetella, Lyme, canine influenza and rattlesnake toxoid, depending on lifestyle and location.
- Most adult dogs that received the full booster series as puppies should be revaccinated every one to three years, based on a lifestyle risk assessment.

## Puppy series
- For most puppies, the final vaccination in the series is given at 16 weeks of age or older (AVMA). VCA describes three doses about three to four weeks apart, starting at six to eight weeks.
- AVMA: a parvovirus booster is recommended within one year after the last dose in the initial series, and afterward boosters are generally recommended every three years.
- Vaccine schedules can be adjusted, so your vet should confirm the plan for your dog.

## Suggested tasks
These are suggested defaults, not statements from the sources.
- Vaccination booster appointment, 45 minutes, high priority.
- Wellness exam, 60 minutes, high priority.
