# Dog Vaccinations and Preventive Care

## Vaccines
- Core vaccines for dogs are rabies, distemper, parvovirus and adenovirus (often combined as DHPP or DAPP). Additional vaccines, such as Bordetella or Lyme, depend on lifestyle and location.
- Puppies usually start their series at 6-8 weeks old, with boosters every 2-4 weeks until about 16 weeks.
- Adult dogs get boosters on a schedule set by the vet, often every 1-3 years depending on the vaccine and local law. Rabies vaccination is legally required in most places.

## Parasite prevention
- Heartworm prevention is typically given monthly all year, and dogs should be tested for heartworm yearly.
- Flea and tick prevention is usually given monthly (or as directed for the product) and is most important in warm months, though many vets recommend year-round use.
- Dogs should have a stool check for intestinal parasites at least once a year.

## Vet visits
- Healthy adult dogs should have a wellness exam at least once a year.
- Puppies and seniors need more frequent visits (see the puppy and senior guides).

## Suggested tasks
- Monthly heartworm and flea/tick preventive, 5 minutes, repeats monthly (use weekly or daily recurrence as the nearest option), high priority.
- Annual wellness exam, 60 minutes, high priority.
- Vaccination booster appointment, 45 minutes, high priority.
