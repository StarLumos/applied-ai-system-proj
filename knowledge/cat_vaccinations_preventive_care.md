# Cat Vaccinations and Preventive Care

## Vaccines
- The AAFP panel recommends that all household cats kept indoors at all times receive vaccines for panleukopenia, feline herpesvirus, calicivirus (together often called FVRCP) and rabies.
- Kittens receive a series of vaccines over a 12 to 16 week period, beginning at 6 to 8 weeks of age.
- Feline leukemia (FeLV) is a core vaccine for cats under one year old, and non-core for cats one year and older that have no potential for exposure to infected cats.
- Frequency of adult boosters depends on the duration of protection of each vaccine. Ask your vet for your cat's schedule.

## Parasite prevention
- International Cat Care recommends routine preventative flea control for cats. Ask your vet which product and schedule suit your cat.

## Suggested tasks
These are suggested defaults, not statements from the sources.
- Kitten vaccination visit, 45 minutes, high priority.
- Vaccination booster appointment, 45 minutes, high priority.
- Flea preventive, 5 minutes, as the product directs, high priority.
