# Cat Vaccinations and Preventive Care

## Vaccines
- Core vaccines for cats are rabies and FVRCP (feline viral rhinotracheitis, calicivirus and panleukopenia).
- Kittens usually start at 6-8 weeks, with boosters every 3-4 weeks until about 16 weeks.
- The feline leukemia (FeLV) vaccine is recommended for kittens and for adult cats at risk, such as those that go outdoors or live with FeLV-positive cats.
- Adult booster schedules are set by the vet, commonly every 1-3 years depending on the vaccine.

## Parasite prevention
- Flea prevention is recommended for most cats, including indoor cats, since fleas can come in on people and other pets.
- Ask your vet about heartworm prevention, which is also relevant for indoor cats in many areas, and about deworming.

## Vet visits
- Healthy adult cats should have a wellness exam at least once a year. Cats hide illness well, so regular checks are important.
- Kittens need several visits in the first four months.

## Suggested tasks
- Annual wellness exam, 60 minutes, high priority.
- Flea/parasite preventive, 5 minutes, monthly or as the product directs, high priority.
- Kitten vaccination visit, 45 minutes, high priority.
