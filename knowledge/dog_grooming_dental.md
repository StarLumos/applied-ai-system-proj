# Dog Grooming, Nails and Dental Care

## Brushing and bathing
- Short-coated dogs may need brushing about once a week. Long-coated or double-coated dogs often need brushing several times a week, and daily during heavy shedding seasons.
- Bathe as needed, commonly every 1-3 months, using a dog-specific shampoo. Over-bathing can dry out the skin.

## Nails
- Trim nails about every 3-4 weeks. If you can hear nails clicking on a hard floor, they are probably too long.
- Overgrown nails can be painful and change how a dog walks.

## Ears
- Check ears weekly for redness, odor or discharge. Dogs with floppy ears or that swim often are more prone to infections. Clean only with a vet-approved cleaner.

## Teeth
- Brushing daily with dog toothpaste (never human toothpaste) is ideal; several times a week is still helpful.
- Most dogs show signs of dental disease by age 3 if teeth are not cared for. Ask your vet about professional cleanings.

## Suggested tasks
- Brush coat, 10-15 minutes, weekly, low priority (more often for long or double coats).
- Brush teeth, 5 minutes, daily, medium priority.
- Nail trim, 15 minutes, every 3-4 weeks, low priority.
- Ear check, 5 minutes, weekly, low priority.
